Make Safer Digital Decisions

AI TrustLens aims to help people understand possible digital risks, recognize warning signs, and make more informed decisions online.

Results are indicators, not guarantees. A website or message cannot be confirmed safe or fraudulent from limited information alone.

Stay Safe Online

  • Never share your OTP or passwords with anyone.
  • Always verify website links before clicking.
  • Avoid unknown payment requests and suspicious offers.
  • Enable two-factor authentication on your accounts.
  • Report suspicious messages and websites.
  • Keep your apps and devices updated regularly.
